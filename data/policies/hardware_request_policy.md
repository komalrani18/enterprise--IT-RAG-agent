# Hardware Request & Replacement Policy

**Document ID:** IT-POL-011
**Last Updated:** 2025-08-15
**Owner:** IT Operations

## 1. Standard Issue Equipment
New hires receive a standard laptop (choice of two models per role type), a monitor,
keyboard, mouse, and headset as part of onboarding, provisioned before their start
date via the IT Onboarding workflow.

## 2. Requesting Additional or Upgraded Hardware
Requests for additional monitors, upgraded laptops, or peripherals beyond the standard
kit require manager approval and are submitted via the Hardware Request Portal.
Standard turnaround is 5-7 business days, subject to inventory; custom/high-spec
requests (e.g., GPU workstations for ML engineers) may take 3-4 weeks and require
budget sign-off from the requesting department's director.

## 3. Loaner Equipment
Employees traveling or awaiting repair may request loaner laptops from the IT Help
Desk on a first-come, first-served basis, for a maximum of 15 business days.

## 4. Repairs & Warranty
Hardware issues under manufacturer warranty are handled directly by IT Operations at
no cost to the employee or department. Accidental damage outside of warranty (e.g.,
liquid damage, drops) may incur a department chargeback per the Asset Damage
Chargeback Schedule, unless covered under the company's accidental damage protection
plan.

## 5. Offboarding
All company hardware must be returned to IT Operations within 5 business days of an
employee's last day, via the office IT desk or a prepaid shipping label for remote
employees. Failure to return equipment may result in a chargeback of the asset's
depreciated value.
